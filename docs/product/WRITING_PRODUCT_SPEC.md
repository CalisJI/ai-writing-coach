# ORENA — WRITING PRODUCT SPECIFICATION

Status: Human product specification  
Languages: EN + ZH  
Core idea: Writing coaches revision and language growth, not one opaque score.

## Tasks
Free writing, opinion, short response, summary, email/message, story, media response, Reading response, vocabulary challenge and exam-style tasks where appropriate.

## Evaluation
Separate task fulfillment, organization/coherence, grammar, vocabulary, clarity/naturalness, mechanics and level-appropriate language only where justified.

## Inline feedback
Distinct underlines/highlights for grammar, vocabulary/word choice, mechanics, clarity, phrase/collocation and style. Clicking a span explains the issue in THIS context, why it matters, corrected alternatives and examples.

## Grammar quality rule
Do not call a sentence absolutely ungrammatical when it can be grammatical in another context. Distinguish impossible, possible-but-wrong-for-intended-meaning, unnatural, register mismatch and better alternative. Chinese explanations must avoid oversimplified rules, including inaccurate treatment of 了.

## Revision loop
Draft → feedback → learner edits → recheck → compare improvement → save useful mistakes/phrases → future review. Do not encourage blind copying of a rewritten model answer.

## Model answer
Label as an example, explain key choices, adapt to learner level and do not imply only one answer is correct.

## Connected learning
Prompts may come from Listening, Reading, Speaking or saved vocabulary. Useful mistakes/phrases feed Library/Active Recall, Grammar review and future Writing.

## Acceptance
Explainable scoring, actionable inline feedback, revision loop, EN/ZH quality, context-sensitive explanations, vocabulary/grammar handoffs and web/native parity.
